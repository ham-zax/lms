# Source quality rubric

Use this rubric when deciding what may support a pattern claim.

| Class | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O | Official NBEMS bulletin/notice/blueprint | Authoritative for exam structure, rules, timing, syllabus and marks. Not evidence of actual recalled question wording. |
| R1 | Inspected full or near-full recall PDF from the originating provider | Primary material for stem/archetype/difficulty analysis, subject to recall error. A direct PDF link alone does not establish completeness. |
| R2 | Substantial independent public recall article/page | Useful pattern evidence; preserve provider provenance and avoid treating it as a full paper unless completeness is established. |
| R3 | Small public preview/sample from a larger recall bank | Useful for examples only. Do not estimate whole-paper frequencies from the sample. |
| R4 | Verified mirror/re-host/duplicate of another provider recall | Do not count as independent corroboration. Keep for access redundancy only. |
| R5 | Landing page, gated download, registration page, uninspected direct PDF or unverified listing | Discovery lead only until the underlying document is obtained and inspected. |

## Rules

1. Never call R1-R5 material an official NBEMS question paper.
2. Frequency statistics should use only sources whose completeness is known well enough for the denominator.
3. Deduplicate the same reconstructed sitting across providers before counting questions.
4. Preserve both the provider label and a later canonical sitting ID; do not merge June/July or December/January labels solely because they look adjacent.
5. Validate medical answers independently when answer accuracy matters. Recall-provider answer keys are not authoritative.
