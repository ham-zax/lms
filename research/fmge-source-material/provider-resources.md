# FMGE provider and recall resources

This catalog tracks public FMGE recall material, sample pages, and commercial-provider exam-pattern resources that can help us model question style and difficulty.

## Use rule

Use these resources to learn **patterns, topic mix, difficulty, clinical framing, image usage, and exam UX**. Do not copy paid/proprietary question banks or reproduce whole third-party PDFs into this repository. Where a provider publishes a free public recall video or sample page, keep the source URL and derive our own independently authored questions from the underlying medical concept.

## Recommended research workflow

1. For each recall session, record only the **medical concept, subject, stem archetype, image/diagram use, and difficulty**.
2. Cross-check the medical fact against authoritative textbooks/guidelines rather than trusting recall answer keys blindly.
3. Generate a fresh question with new wording and new distractors.
4. Tag each generated item with source provenance such as:
   - `source_type: recall_pattern`
   - `source_provider: Cerebellum`
   - `source_session: FMGE Jan 2026`
   - `confidence: low|medium|high`
5. Never describe an independently authored item as an "official FMGE question."

This gives us a useful recall-derived training/reference corpus without embedding commercial question-bank content in the codebase.
