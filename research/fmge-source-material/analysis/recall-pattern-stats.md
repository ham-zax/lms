## Method checks

- Subject classifier: 5-fold accuracy 51% on 1001 provider-labelled questions.
- Image detector (stem wording) against FMGEPrep's own image flag on 38 flagged samples: recall 74%, precision 90%. Recall PDFs describe the image in the stem ("image given below"), which is what the detector reads.
- Stem type, task, negative and calculation are rule-based on the stem text; see the regexes in the script.
