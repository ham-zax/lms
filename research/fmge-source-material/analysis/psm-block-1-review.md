All 50 keyed answers are medically defensible; none is simply wrong. The problems are exam style:

1. **Stems point at the notes** (37 items): "shown in the notes", "according to the source", "on the AEFI chart". Real FMGE tests the fact itself.
2. **Stated premises** (36, 40, 45, 48): the stem hands over the fact that decides the answer.
3. **Two questions in one** (41, 42, 44, 46): "which pair/combination…" items that join two unrelated recalls. FMGE rarely does this; it is artificial Tier 3.
4. **Questions answer each other** in the same section:
   - Q43's stem gives "16-20 vials", which is the answer to Q12;
   - Q49's stem gives "1 lakh IU/mL", which is the answer to Q15;
   - Q42's options repeat the ASHA visit counts tested in Q29;
   - Q9 and Q35 use the same four COVID vaccines as options.
5. **Weak distractors** (27, 43, 48): options no candidate would pick ("Apply concentrated alcohol without water", "Walk-in cooler" for a subcentre, "Substitute live JE vaccine").
6. **Tier labels too high**: Q20, 23, 30, 31, 34 and 35 are single-fact recall, labelled Tier 2.
7. **No EXCEPT items and no images.** Real FMGE has a few of each; Q26 (VVM) and Q33 (ice pack) are natural image items.
8. **Numeric options out of order** (Q13, Q10): FMGE usually lists numbers in ascending order.
