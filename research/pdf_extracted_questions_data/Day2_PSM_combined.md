### Q36 — **C — Administer measles/MR vaccine now**

**Source:** pp.59, 67 | **E2**.
The lyophilized-vaccine page says reconstituted vaccine should be used within **4 hours** and links failure to this handling rule with TSS; the AEFI chart also associates TSS with measles vaccine.

**Concept link:** epidemic threshold + zero-background disease status.

### Q39 — **A — Two IM doses on days 0 and 3**
**Source:** pp.78–79 | **E2**.
The rabies schedule explicitly gives **post-exposure in vaccinated: IM day 0 and day 3**. The wound-care page supplies the initial washing step already completed in the stem.

### Q40 — **C — Wild P2 eradicated while vaccine-derived type 2 remained a concern**

The switch page states that **P2 had been eradicated** before tOPV was changed to bOPV, while the AEFI/OPV notes identify circulating vaccine-derived poliovirus most commonly with type 2.
**Closest distractor:** type 1 removal.

### Q41 — **B — 40/100,000 — maternal care**

### Q42 — **C — 4 antenatal + 6 postnatal**

### Q43 — **A — Vaccine carrier**
**Source:** pp.61, 64 | **E2**.
The subcentre has no fixed storage equipment in the hierarchy, while the vaccine carrier is shown as holding **16–20 vials** for approximately **12–24 hours**.
**Concept link:** service level + equipment capacity/duration.

### Q44 — **D — PNMR — 18/1000**
**Source:** pp.105–106 | **E2**.
The indicator table gives **PNMR = 18**, and the following diagram labels PNMR as the best indicator of **combined maternal and newborn care**.

### Q45 — **B — The elimination level is met**
**Source:** pp.18, 34 | **E2** | Threshold + prevalence/rate logic.
Eight existing cases in 100,000 people correspond to **0.8 per 10,000**, which is below the source's leprosy elimination level of **<1 per 10,000 population**.
**Closest distractor:** C. Globally eradicated.
**Key discriminator:** meeting a prevalence threshold does not establish global eradication.
**Concept link:** convert the population prevalence using the source's measure logic, then compare it with the disease-specific elimination threshold.

### Q46 — **D — Sentinel surveillance + screening**
**Source:** pp.19, 31 | **E2**.
The surveillance page labels sentinel surveillance as a way to identify **hidden/missing cases**, while the iceberg page labels **screening** as the method used to detect the hidden/submerged subclinical pool. They are two different tools, not synonyms.
**Concept link:** surveillance strategy for hidden/missing cases + detection method for subclinical disease.

### Q47 — **D — Rehabilitation**
**Source:** pp.21, 27 | **E2**.
The patient has progressed from impairment to disability and difficulty fulfilling a social role. Tertiary prevention contains **disability limitation and rehabilitation**.

### Q48 — **A — Do not administer Qdenga during pregnancy**
**Source:** pp.55, 77 | **E2**.
Qdenga is labelled **live attenuated**, while pregnancy is listed among contraindications to live vaccination.
**Concept link:** vaccine platform → contraindication rule.

### Q49 — **B — 2 mL**
